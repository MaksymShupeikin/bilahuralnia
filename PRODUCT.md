## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (user's choice), static output. Product catalog lives in one data file so product pages, catalog and cart read the same source.

## Users

- Adults in Ukraine buying premium craft samohon for themselves or, very often, as a gift (gift boxes, sets, oak cases are a large part of the range). They arrive from Instagram/Facebook or search, mostly on phones.
- Secondary: English-speaking visitors (expats, foreign guests, export interest) who need to understand what samohon is and what each bottle tastes like.

## Product Purpose

Online shop and brand home of «Біла Гуральня» (Bila Huralnia), a craft distillery in Ternopil. Success: a visitor picks a bottle or gift set by taste or occasion, sees the price, and places an order for Nova Poshta delivery or pickup without calling first.

## Positioning

Premium samohon reframed as a national craft spirit: double distillation, natural juices and selected grain (elderberry with rye or barley, seven Ukrainian grains), on a label system that reads like a spirits spec sheet. Revival of Ukrainian samohon tradition, not rustic moonshine.

## Operating Context

- Ordering: cart → order form; a manager always calls to confirm details regardless of payment method.
- Payment: cash on pickup; cash on delivery at Nova Poshta (Nova Poshta fees); bank transfer to ТОВ «Біла Гуральня» account; card transfer.
- Delivery: Nova Poshta branches, 1–3 days from order, at Nova Poshta rates.
- Shop / pickup: вул. С. Будного, 7, м. Тернопіль, 46027. Phone +38 (067) 600 03 44, bilahuralnia@gmail.com. Instagram @bilahuralnia, Facebook page.
- Legal: alcohol is not sold to persons under 18; age confirmation is required on entry.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: «Біла Гуральня» / Bila Huralnia; wordmark «Гуральня» with small «Біла» set vertically, tagline «преміальний самогон», round monogram «Г» with a droplet and glass. Existing logo files must be used, not redrawn.
- Bottle label system: cream label, ruled grid with «SINCE 2022», volume and ABV, one colour band per variety. Bottles and labels are the brand's strongest asset.
- Use only the real photos and video from the current site (user decision): studio bottle shots on grey, the dark hero video of bottles, barrel-cellar photos, the four-bottle group shot.
- Standing preference (user chose it over distinctive directions, 2026-10-23): the premium-spirits category standard played straight — dark ground, restrained gold, refined serif display, full-screen film. Quality bar: The Macallan and Nemiroff websites.

## Product Principles

1. Price and a way to buy are always one tap away from any bottle.
2. Choose by taste and occasion, not by scrolling a flat list.
3. The bottle is the hero; the interface stays out of its way.
4. Tradition stated through craft facts (grain, juice, distillation), never folklore costume.
5. Both languages are first-class; nothing hides behind the language switch.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
